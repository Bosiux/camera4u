# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 26, 2026_

Film Camera for Android does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## Camera

Film Camera uses the camera only to show the live preview and take the photos you ask for. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

## Photos

Photos you take are saved to your device's gallery, in Pictures/Film Camera. Film Camera doesn't ask for storage or media permissions: it can add its own photos, but it cannot read the rest of your photos.

## Network

Film Camera connects to GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) only to download the film catalog, film LUT files and their preview images. These requests contain no personal information. GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Analytics, tracking and advertising

Film Camera contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Data stored on your device

Your settings, your film list and downloaded films are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app. If you use Android's device backup, your settings and downloaded films may be included in that backup, which is handled by Google under your account.

## Contact

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/YahiaAngelo/FilmCamera-Android/issues.
